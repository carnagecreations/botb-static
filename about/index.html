About the Rescue

Founded on a simple idea: reptiles deserve better than disposal.

Beauties of the Beasts is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit reptile rescue based in Yuma, Arizona, and we exist for one reason: the animals the system leaves behind. The snake found loose in a garage. The bearded dragon a shelter has no way to house. The tortoise that outgrew an owner who never knew a 100-year commitment was on the table. We show up for all of them.

Reptiles & Bulldogs. No other cats or dogs.

Our Mission

Most reptiles surrendered to general shelters don't survive. We've seen it happen too many times to accept it as normal. So we built something different — a rescue that treats every animal as a long-term commitment, not a number moving through a system. We take in the ones no one else will, stabilize them, and place them only when we're certain the match will hold.

Beauties of the Beasts pairs reptile rescue with a human sanctuary, keeping animals and families together. We provide education, practical resources, emergency support, and safe refuge for reptiles and the people who love them — especially during financial hardship, unemployment, and crisis. No one should have to surrender a beloved animal because they need help. Learn more on our Human Sanctuary page.

Every placement we make is one fewer animal re-surrendered two years from now.
02

What We Handle

Snakes, lizards, turtles, tortoises, and amphibians. We do not accept venomous species or animals requiring permits we do not hold. Specializing is how rescues stay effective, so outside of our dedicated Bunch a Bull Bulldog Rescue, we leave dogs, cats, and other species to the shelters built around them — for now.

03

How We Work

Every animal gets species-correct housing, a structured acclimation period, and a clear care plan before it is listed. We partner with exotic-experienced veterinarians in the Yuma area for medical assessment. Every adopter gets vetted — we are not a pet store. We would rather an animal stay with us than go to the wrong home.

04

How We Started

We run Beauties of the Beasts entirely out of our home in Yuma. It started the way most rescues do — one animal at a time. A surrendered ball python. A neglected bearded dragon. What began as quiet rehoming in spare rooms became a registered 501(c)(3) when we realized how many reptiles were falling through the cracks of a shelter system that was never built for them. Every animal in our care lives in our space. That's a choice — it's how we ensure each one gets the attention it needs.

Along the way, we noticed something else: many of the animals we took in weren't surrendered because someone stopped loving them. They were surrendered because a person was struggling — with a lost job, a medical bill, a move, or a crisis they didn't choose. That observation is why we expanded our mission to include the Human Sanctuary: animal welfare through family stability. Reptile rescue remains central to our work, and we're expanding this new side of it responsibly — only as far as our capacity, training, partnerships, and resources allow.

05

Looking Ahead

We're working to secure a dedicated facility — real space that lets us take in far more than a home can hold today, including dogs and cats beyond our current Bunch a Bull Bulldog Rescue, and a proper building for the Human Sanctuary's own programming. We don't have a move-in date yet, but every gift moves us closer. Read more on our vision page.

Our Standards

The values we will never trade away.

01 / 03

Species-specific care

Ball pythons are not bearded dragons. Our enclosures, diets, and handling protocols reflect that.

02 / 03

Honest placement

We turn down adopters. We ask hard questions. This is how animals stop being rehomed three times.

03 / 03

Judgment-free intake

Surrender is welcomed here, never punished — we'd rather take in an animal than see it dumped.

04 / 04

Clear about our limits

We do not hold federal permits. We do not accept venomous species. Outside of our Bunch a Bull Bulldog Rescue, we don't yet have the space to take on other dogs and cats — knowing what we cannot do today is how we do right by the animals we can. We're working toward a larger facility that changes that.

Reptile Rescue in Yuma, Arizona

The only dedicated reptile rescue in Yuma, AZ

We founded Beauties of the Beasts in 2023 in Yuma, Arizona to address a specific gap: the complete absence of species-appropriate reptile care in the Southwest Arizona rescue ecosystem. General shelters lack the training, equipment, and volunteer expertise to keep reptiles alive under proper husbandry conditions. We built this organization to be the fix.

Volunteers run this 501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 88-2959170, entirely. We take no government funding. Every dollar donated goes directly to animal care — feeders, heating equipment, veterinary referrals, and enclosure supplies. No salaries. No overhead. Nothing skimmed.

Since founding in 2023, we've taken in over 75 animals, including ball pythons, bearded dragons, banded water snakes, tortoises, and other lizard and colubrid species. We've placed the majority in permanent, vetted homes across Arizona and the Southwest. Every adoption goes through a structured review process that evaluates experience level, enclosure readiness, and long-term fit — because our goal is permanent placement, not revolving-door rehoming.

Our rescue serves Yuma County and the broader Arizona border region, and accepts surrenders from across the Southwest on a case-by-case basis. Looking to surrender a reptile, adopt a reptile, or donate to reptile rescue in Arizona? We're here. Every animal in our care is living proof that reptiles deserve the same standard of care as any other species.

Scope

We focus on reptiles, amphibians, and bulldogs through our dedicated Bunch a Bull Bulldog Rescue. For other dogs, cats, and species, contact HSOY (Humane Society of Yuma) or Yuma County Animal Control.
