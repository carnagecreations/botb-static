Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Who We Are

Beauties of the Beasts Animal Rescue is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization (EIN: 88-2959170) based in Yuma, Arizona. We rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome surrendered reptiles and amphibians across the Southwest. You can reach us at admin@yumareptiles.com.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Submit an adoption application (name, address, contact details, housing situation)
  • Complete a surrender intake form (contact details, information about your animal)
  • Contact us by email
  • Make a donation through our third-party processor (Zeffy)
  • Sign up for our newsletter

We do not collect payment information directly. Donations are processed by Zeffy, subject to their own privacy policy.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Process adoption and surrender applications
  • Respond to your inquiries
  • Send organizational updates if you opt in
  • Fulfill our nonprofit mission

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

4. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. This data is aggregated and anonymous. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

We do not display advertising or use advertising cookies. We do not participate in AdSense or other ad networks.

5. Data Retention

We retain application records for as long as necessary to fulfill our rescue operations. You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing us at admin@yumareptiles.com.

6. Third-Party Links

Our site links to third-party platforms including Zeffy (donations), Instagram, and Facebook. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these sites and encourage you to review their policies.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. California and Arizona Residents

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal data we collect and to request its deletion. Arizona residents may have similar rights under applicable state law. As a small nonprofit that collects minimal data, we honor all reasonable deletion and access requests. Contact us at admin@yumareptiles.com to exercise these rights.
