What We're Building Toward

We're working toward securing a dedicated facility — space that would let us expand well beyond what we can do out of our home today, including taking on more dogs and cats beyond our current Bunch a Bull Bulldog Rescue. Nothing official yet, but it's the direction we're headed, and every donation gets us there sooner.

Human Sanctuary

Your Donation Helps Keep Families Together

Beyond direct animal care, your gift also supports the Human Sanctuary — the people-support side of our work, dedicated to keeping animals and families together during hardship.

Emergency reptile food and husbandry supplies

Safe temporary care for reptiles and select exotic companion animals

Enclosures, storage, quarantine supplies, cleaning materials, heating, lighting, and care equipment

Surrender-prevention supplies

Community education and resource-navigation materials

Digital access and workforce-support programming

Volunteer and foster training

Donations support animal care, supplies, and education. They do not fund human housing, cash assistance, legal assistance, or professional counseling.

Scope Notice

Beauties of the Beasts is a reptile rescue, with one exception: our Bunch a Bull Bulldog Rescue. If you're looking to donate to a dog or cat rescue outside of bulldogs, we'd be grateful if you supported one of the shelters listed on our contact page.

Beauties of the Beasts

A 501(c)(3) reptile rescue based in Yuma, Arizona. 100% volunteer-run. Every dollar goes to the animals.

Scope

We focus on reptiles, amphibians, and bulldogs through our dedicated Bunch a Bull Bulldog Rescue. For other dogs and cats, contact HSOY or Yuma County Animal Control.
