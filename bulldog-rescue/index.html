A Program Within the Rescue

Bunch a Bull Bulldog Rescue.

Beauties of the Beasts is a reptile rescue — with one exception. Molly Mayer pulls bulldogs from high-kill shelters, often with only hours left on the clock, and places them into vetted foster homes. She built this program at another 501(c)(3) first — but wanted to do things differently, so she moved it here, joined our board of directors, and is now in a position to save even more lives.

How It Works

Pulled from high-kill shelters. Placed in real homes.

Bulldogs and bully breeds are some of the most at-risk dogs in high-kill shelters — their size, reputation, and medical needs often mean shorter timelines before euthanasia.

Molly tracks intake at high-kill shelters and drives out the moment we have a foster home ready. Every dog she pulls leaves with a plan already in place: a vet intake, a foster placement, and a path to adoption.

This program runs on foster availability, full stop. More foster homes means more dogs we can reach in time. If you have the space, this is where you're needed most.

Foster-to-Adopt for Bulldogs

Most bulldogs enter as foster-to-adopt while they finish spay/neuter surgery or remaining vaccinations. You foster, we cover the medical care, and adoption finalizes once they're fully cleared — so you get to know each other first, and they get to feel safe from day one.

Medical Support

Bulldogs often arrive with health needs — respiratory issues, skin conditions, joint problems. We cover every dog's medical costs from intake through adoption. Foster families never pay out of pocket.

Contact Molly

Questions about the bulldog program? Email Molly, our Bulldog Rescue Lead, directly at molly@yumareptiles.com.

Why Bulldogs

Molly's breed. Molly's mission.

Bulldogs have always been Molly's heart dogs. She built this program at another 501(c)(3) first — but wanted to run it differently, so she moved the work to Beauties of the Beasts and joined our board of directors.

She has more say, more resources, and more reach than she had before — which means more dogs saved.

That move became Bunch a Bull. It's specific to one breed today because that's where Molly's expertise and hands-on capacity are strongest — not because we think any animal deserves it less.

Our belief

Every animal deserves a home. Right now, we're breed-specific by necessity, not by belief — and we hope to change that soon.

Beauties of the Beasts

A 501(c)(3) reptile rescue based in Yuma, Arizona. Founded 2023. 100% volunteer-run — every dollar goes directly to the animals.

Scope

We focus on reptiles, amphibians, and bulldogs through this dedicated Bunch a Bull Bulldog Rescue. For other dogs, cats, and species, contact HSOY (Humane Society of Yuma) or Yuma County Animal Control.
